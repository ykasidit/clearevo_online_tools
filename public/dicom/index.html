DICOM Viewer by ClearEvo.com 

      
Free, open-source DICOM viewer - runs 100% in your browser

    

    
Written by Kasidit Yusuf, co-founder of ClearEvo, to open his own CT scan CD on his phone - then given away free to everyone as a gift from ClearEvo. No ads, no sign-up, no upsell. The demo scans are his own head CT and MRI.

    
© 2026 Kasidit Yusuf. Free software under the GNU General Public License, version 2.

    
Provided “as is”, with NO WARRANTY and no support - without even the implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose. Not a medical device: for personal viewing of your own scans, not for diagnosis - that belongs to your radiologist and their certified tools.

    
Privacy: your images are decoded on your device and are never uploaded (Google Analytics for visit stats only). How to verify →

    
Source code: github.com/ykasidit/clearevo_online_tools - issues and pull requests welcome.
