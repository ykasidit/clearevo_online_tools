BatRay by ClearEvo.com 
JK (JiKong) BMS monitor over Web Bluetooth
Turn an old phone, tablet or notebook into a JK BMS monitor by opening one URL - no Raspberry Pi, no ESP32, nothing to flash.
Share live sends readings to your viewers encrypted on this device with a key that exists only in the share link, so nobody in between can read them. Without sharing, nothing leaves your device. History charts are still to come.
